# RapidPulse Durban — Project Overview
## 🛠️ Built With

*   **React Native** — For building the cross-platform mobile user interface.
*   **Expo** — For rapid mobile development, testing, and deployment.
*   **JavaScript (ES6+)** — The core programming language used for application logic.

## 1. Project Description

RapidPulse Durban is a mobile emergency response prototype designed to support people in Durban, KwaZulu-Natal, during emergencies while they wait for professional emergency services to arrive. The app aims to provide access to basic first-aid guidance, emergency safety information, and community-based emergency support.

The platform is designed to connect people experiencing an emergency with the nearest available community responder who has first-aid training or paramedic qualifications, helping them receive assistance while waiting for professional emergency services. It also aims to provide a way to record essential patient information and track the progress of an emergency request.

RapidPulse Durban also includes a gender-based violence (GBV) emergency alert concept designed for people who may be in immediate danger. The feature aims to alert appropriate support responders so they can identify the person's need for urgent help and, where location sharing is available and consented to, locate them as quickly as possible.

The overall goal of RapidPulse Durban is to bring emergency guidance, trained community responder support, and emergency alert functionality together in one accessible mobile platform, helping people access assistance more quickly while professional emergency services are on their way.

## 2. Problem Statement

During emergencies, people may struggle to access immediate guidance, communicate important patient information, or identify available support while waiting for professional emergency services. RapidPulse Durban aims to address these challenges through a centralised mobile application that provides useful information and demonstrates how a community-based emergency response network could operate.
## 3. Project Objectives

- Provide users with accessible first-aid guidance and emergency safety information while they wait for professional emergency services.
- Allow users to register basic contact and medical information to support emergency assistance.
- Develop an emergency request and response tracking process to help users follow the progress of their requests.
- Maintain a response history showing previous emergency requests, dates, locations, and response statuses.
- Support connections between people experiencing emergencies and nearby community members with first-aid training or paramedic qualifications.
- Include a GBV emergency alert feature designed to help people in danger request urgent assistance and alert appropriate responders to their situation.
- Encourage trained community members to register as responders and provide support within their qualifications and abilities.
- Promote emergency preparedness, responsible community participation, and cooperation with professional emergency services.

## 4. Main Features

**User Registration and Sign-In:** Provides a registration interface for patients and a demo sign-in process for accessing the application.

**Emergency Response Dashboard:** Displays a simulated Durban response map, a standby team indicator, and an emergency request button to demonstrate how users could request assistance during an emergency.

**Community Responder Support:** Aims to connect people experiencing emergencies with nearby community members who have first-aid training or paramedic qualifications, allowing them to provide appropriate assistance while professional emergency services are on their way.

**Response Tracking:** Demonstrates how users could view responder details, estimated arrival times, request statuses, and emergency notification information.

**First-Aid Guide:** Provides basic emergency safety guidance for situations such as severe bleeding and an unresponsive person, helping users understand appropriate initial actions while waiting for professional assistance.

**Response History:** Displays previous demo emergency requests, including dates, locations, statuses, and optional reference numbers, so users can review their recorded activity.

**Community Responder Registration:** Allows interested community members to submit their contact details, location or service area, training information, responder preferences, and availability.

**Gender-Based Violence (GBV) Emergency Alert:** Includes a panic-button concept designed to help people who may be in danger request urgent assistance and alert appropriate support responders. With secure location-sharing functionality, responders could identify the person's location and find them more quickly.

**Emergency Preparedness and Community Support:** Brings emergency information, trained community participation, and response tracking together in one platform to demonstrate how technology could support people during emergencies.

## 5. Technology Stack

- **React Native:** Mobile application interface and components.
- **JavaScript:** Application logic, state management, and interactive features.
- **Expo:** Development and testing environment.
- **React Hooks (`useState`):** Management of screens, registration forms, simulated requests, and response history.
- **StyleSheet:** Layout, colours, typography, and visual styling.

## 6. Security and Privacy Considerations

The project recognises the importance of protecting patient information and ensuring responsible emergency response management. Future development should include secure authentication, encrypted communication, appropriate access controls, secure data storage, privacy protections, responder identity verification, and audit logs.

Medical information and location data should only be collected when necessary and handled with appropriate consent and safeguards. Emergency and GBV alerts must be connected to verified services and tested before being used in real situations.

## 7. Current Project Status

RapidPulse Durban is currently a **working prototype in development**. Its interface includes registration forms, a simulated emergency response workflow, first-aid guidance, responder applications, and response history.

The current prototype does not dispatch real emergency responders, send applications to a server, verify user accounts, share live locations, or deliver real emergency notifications. These capabilities require further development, backend integration, security testing, and collaboration with appropriate emergency service providers.

## 8. Future Development

Planned improvements include:

- Integrating a secure backend and database.
- Implementing real user authentication and account verification.
- Connecting to verified emergency response services.
- Enabling consent-based location sharing and live response tracking.
- Developing secure emergency contact and GBV alert functionality.
- Introducing responder verification, approval, and availability management.
- Conducting functional, security, privacy, and usability testing.

## 9. Expected Impact

RapidPulse Durban aims to demonstrate how mobile technology could support emergency preparedness, improve access to basic first-aid information, organise community responder participation, and provide clearer visibility of emergency request progress. With appropriate partnerships, secure infrastructure, and professional oversight, the project could develop into a practical tool supporting emergency response initiatives in Durban.

**Project summary:** RapidPulse Durban is a React Native and Expo mobile application prototype exploring how emergency information, simulated response tracking, patient registration, and community support features can be brought together in one accessible platform.
